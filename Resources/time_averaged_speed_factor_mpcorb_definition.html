Time-Averaged Speed Factor (2E(e)/π) in the Context of the MPCORB Database

Scientific Definition, Complete Elliptic Integral of the Second Kind & Perimeter Scaling, and Derivation from MPCORB

1. Fundamental Astrodynamical & Analytical Definition

Definition & Nomenclature: In two-body Keplerian celestial mechanics, the time-averaged speed factor (also termed elliptic arc-length factor or temporal mean velocity ratio, denoted f⟨v⟩(e) = 2E(e) / π) is the dimensionless function of orbital eccentricity e (0 ≤ e < 1) that scales the mean circular orbital velocity vc = √(μ⊙/a) to the exact time-averaged orbital speed ⟨v⟩t over one complete revolution, and simultaneously scales the circle circumference 2πa to the exact ellipse perimeter Lellipse.

Integrating instantaneous orbital speed v = ds/dt over one orbital period P = 2πa / vc yields the total arc length Lellipse = 4a E(e), where E(e) = ∫0π/2 √(1 − e2 sin2θ) dθ is the complete elliptic integral of the second kind with elliptic modulus k = e:

f⟨v⟩(e) ≡ ⟨v⟩tvc = Lellipse2πa = 2 E(e)π = 2π ∫0π/2 √(1 − e2 sin2θ) dθ

Series Expansion, Bounds & Analytical Properties

  • Strict Bounds (2/π < f⟨v⟩(e) ≤ 1): At e = 0, E(0) = π/2 so f⟨v⟩(0) = 1. As e → 1−, E(1) = 1, so the factor approaches the finite lower limit 2/π ≈ 0.63661977 (reflecting the degenerate back-and-forth length 4a divided by 2πa).
  • Ivory–Bessel Power Series: Expanding the integrand in powers of e2 yields the rapidly convergent series:
    2 E(e)π = 1 − 14e2 − 364e4 − 5256e6 − 17516384e8 − …
  • Strict Inequality Ordering: Because f⟨v⟩(e) < 1 for all e > 0, the true time-averaged orbital speed ⟨v⟩t is strictly smaller than the energy-equivalent circular speed vc (vQ < ⟨v⟩t < vc < vq).
  • Quadratic Arithmetic-Geometric Mean (AGM) Evaluation: 2E(e)/π is evaluated to full IEEE-754 double precision in 5–7 iterations using the Gauss–Legendre AGM algorithm without numerical quadrature.

3. Specific Status and Derivation in the MPCORB Catalog

  • Direct Evaluation from Eccentricity e: Computed directly from columns 71–79 (F9.7) of MPCORB.DAT via AGM evaluation of 2E(e)/π for bound orbits (0 ≤ e < 1).
  • Simultaneous Perimeter & Mean-Speed Derivation: Multiplying vc by 2E(e)/π gives the exact temporal mean speed ⟨v⟩t, while multiplying 2πa by the same factor gives the exact orbital circumference Lellipse.
  • Meteoroid Stream & Cosmic-Dust Collision Flux Modeling: In interplanetary dust and meteoroid stream dynamics derived from MPCORB.DAT parent bodies, ⟨v⟩t and Lellipse govern spatial number density along the orbit (λ(s) ∝ 1/v(s)) and mean collisional sweeping rates.
