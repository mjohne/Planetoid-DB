Aphelion Velocity Factor (√[(1 − e)/(1 + e)]) in the Context of the MPCORB Database

Scientific Definition, Dimensionless Attenuation of Mean Circular Speed at Aphelion, and Derivation from MPCORB

1. Fundamental Astrodynamical & Kinematic Definition

Definition & Nomenclature: In two-body Keplerian celestial mechanics, the aphelion velocity factor (also termed apocentric speed reduction factor or minimum-to-circular velocity ratio, denoted fQ(e) = √[(1 − e) / (1 + e)]) is the dimensionless, monotonically decreasing function of orbital eccentricity e (0 ≤ e < 1) that scales the mean circular orbital velocity vc = √(μ⊙/a) to the instantaneous heliocentric aphelion velocity vQ.

Dividing the aphelion speed vQ = √[μ⊙(1 − e)/(a(1 + e))] by the mean circular speed vc = √(μ⊙/a) isolates the dimensionless attenuation factor:

fQ(e) ≡ vQvc = √ 1 − e 1 + e = √qQ = 1 − e√(1 − e2) = 1fq(e)

Kinematic & Asymptotic Properties

  • Strict Boundedness on 0 ≤ e < 1: Unlike fq(e), fQ(e) is bounded across the entire elliptic domain with 0 < fQ(e) ≤ 1, attaining fQ(0) = 1 for circular orbits and approaching 0 as e → 1−.
  • Small-Eccentricity Expansion: fQ(e) = 1 − e + ½e2 − ½e3 + …, indicating an approximate 100e% reduction below vc for low-eccentricity asteroids.
  • Reciprocal & Inverse-Anomaly Identities: fQ(e) = 1 / fq(e) = tan(E/2) / tan(ν/2), converting true anomaly ν directly into eccentric anomaly E.
  • Kinetic Energy Suppression at Aphelion: Specific orbital kinetic energy at aphelion is suppressed by KQ / Kc = [fQ(e)]2 = (1 − e)/(1 + e), explaining why minor planets spend the majority of their orbital period near aphelion (Kepler’s second law).

2. Contrast Among Velocity Scaling Factors

Velocity Factor Formula Range on 0 ≤ e < 1 Role Relative to vc
Aphelion Velocity Factor (fQ) √[(1 − e)/(1 + e)] (0, 1] Attenuates vc to minimum orbital speed vQ
Perihelion Velocity Factor (fq) √[(1 + e)/(1 − e)] [1, ∞) Amplifies vc to maximum orbital speed vq (1/fQ)
Time-Averaged Speed Factor (f⟨v⟩) 2 E(e) / π (2/π, 1] Attenuates vc to temporal mean speed ⟨v⟩t

3. Specific Status and Derivation in the MPCORB Catalog

  • Direct Evaluation from Eccentricity e: Computed directly from columns 71–79 (F9.7) of MPCORB.DAT via sqrt((1.0 − e) / (1.0 + e)) for bound elliptic orbits (0 ≤ e < 1).
  • Bound-Orbit Restriction (e < 1): Because parabolic (e = 1) and hyperbolic (e > 1) objects in MPCORB.DAT have no aphelion, fQ(e) is undefined for e ≥ 1 (whereas hyperbolic excess speed at infinity is v∞ = √(−μ⊙/a)).
  • Outpost Mission & Plane-Change Energetics: Low fQ(e) identifies high-eccentricity asteroids whose aphelion speed is so small that large inclination changes (Δvplane = 2vQ sin(Δi/2)) require minimal propellant impulse.
