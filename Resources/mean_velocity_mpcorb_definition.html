Mean Velocity

Time-Averaged Keplerian Orbital Speed Over One Sidereal Revolution

1. Formal Definition

In the context of minor planet orbital characterization from MPCORB data, the mean orbital velocity (v̄ or ⟨v⟩) is the time-averaged scalar orbital speed of a minor planet over one complete Keplerian revolution of sidereal period P. Mathematically, it equals the total geometric perimeter (arc length L) of the osculating ellipse divided by the orbital period P.

In astrodynamical literature, care must be taken to distinguish the exact time-averaged orbital speed v̄ = L / P from the circular reference velocity vc = √(μ / a) = n a (the speed of a hypothetical body on a circular orbit of radius a). Because an eccentric body spends disproportionately more time near aphelion (where speed is lowest) according to Kepler's second law, the true time-averaged orbital velocity v̄ is strictly less than vc for all e > 0.

2. Mathematical Formulation from MPCORB Elements

From the osculating semimajor axis a (AU, MPCORB.DAT cols 93–103) and eccentricity e (cols 71–79), the time-averaged speed over one full orbital period P = 2π √(a3 / μ) is defined by the path integral:

v̄ = (1 / P) ∫0P v(t) dt = L / P = (4a E(e)) / (2π √(a3 / μ)) = (2 / π) √(μ / a) · E(e) (Eq. 1)

where:

  • L = 4a E(e) is the exact circumference of an ellipse with semimajor axis a and eccentricity e.
  • E(e) = ∫0π/2 √(1 − e2 sin2φ) dφ is the complete elliptic integral of the second kind (not to be confused with the eccentric anomaly E).
  • μ = k2 is the heliocentric gravitational parameter (k = 0.01720209895 AU3/2 day−1).

Power-Series Expansion & Algebraic Approximations

Expanding E(e) as a Maclaurin power series in e2 yields the exact analytical series for v̄:

v̄ = √(μ / a) · [ 1 − (1/4)e2 − (3/64)e4 − (5/256)e6 − (175/16384)e8 − … ] (Eq. 2)

For high-speed evaluation across large catalogs such as MPCORB.DAT (>1.4 million objects), Ramanujan's second approximation for elliptic arc length provides sub-part-per-million accuracy up to e ≈ 0.9 without iterative elliptic integral quadrature:

v̄ ≈ √(μ / a) · (1 + b/a) · [ 1 + (3hR) / (10 + √(4 − 3hR)) ],   b/a = √(1 − e2),   hR = ((a − b)/(a + b))2 (Eq. 3)
