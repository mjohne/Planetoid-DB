Number of Observations (Nobs, Columns 118–122)

Accepted Optical Astrometric Position Count (FORTRAN I5) & Covariance Scaling in MPCORB.DAT

1. Formal Definition

In the Minor Planet Center Orbit Database (MPCORB), the Number of Observations (Nobs, officially specified by the IAU Minor Planet Center as the “Number of observations” field in columns 118–122, 1-indexed, right-justified integer formatted as FORTRAN I5, with 0-based substring slice [117..122]) denotes the total count of accepted, non-rejected optical astrometric position pairs (αk, δk) included in the differential least-squares orbit determination:

Nobs = int(Record[117 : 122]) ∈ ℕ0,    νdof = 2 Nobs − pfit (Eq. 1)

where:

  • Each optical astrometric record in an 80-column MPC1992 or ADES submission provides two scalar angular observables—Right Ascension (αk) and Declination (δk)—so Nobs observations supply 2 Nobs scalar residual equations to the normal-equations matrix AT W A.
  • pfit is the number of solved dynamical parameters (typically pfit = 6 osculating Keplerian/Cartesian state components at epoch t0, or pfit = 7 when solving simultaneously for the transverse non-gravitational Yarkovsky parameter A2).
  • νdof = 2 Nobs − pfit is the statistical degrees of freedom of the orbit fit: Nobs = 3 (2 Nobs = 6) corresponds to an exact, zero-degree-of-freedom deterministic boundary-value orbit (Gauss/Laplace method), whereas Nobs ≥ 4 yields an overdetermined least-squares regression.

2. Statistical & Astrodynamical Properties

  • Covariance Scaling (Nobs−1/2 vs. Arc Length Δtarc−3/2): Under uncorrelated Gaussian astrometric errors of standard deviation σast, the formal covariance matrix of the fitted orbital state &mathbf{x}0 is
    Σx = (AT W A)−1 ∝ σast2Nobs · G(Δtarc, geometry) (Eq. 2)
    Consequently, standard errors on the orbital elements decrease with 1 / √Nobs when observations are distributed across independent nights/oppositions, whereas dense same-night bursts of hundreds of exposures suffer from correlated systematic catalog/timing errors and yield diminishing returns until Δtarc (columns 128–136) expands.
  • Exclusion of Outliers & Radar/Spacecraft Measurements:
    • Rejected Optical Outliers: Observations exceeding the pipeline's rejection threshold (typically > 2.5σ–3.5σ in post-fit O−C residuals, or flagged as misidentifications) are excluded from the differential correction and are not counted in Nobs.
    • Optical vs. Radar Counts: In standard MPC MPCORB.DAT records, columns 118–122 report the count of optical astrometric observations; when JPL Horizons solutions (e.g., JPL 218 for (99942) Apophis) are imported, delay-Doppler radar range/Doppler measurements are incorporated into the force-model fit alongside the Nobs optical positions.
  • Relation to the MPC Condition Code (U Parameter): In MPCORB.DAT, the orbit uncertainty parameter U ∈ [0..9] (encoded in columns 106–107) depends jointly on Nobs, Nopp, Δtarc, and σrms. Low Nobs (≤ 6) on a short single-opposition arc almost invariably yields U = 7–9 (high ephemeris uncertainty).

3. Fixed-Width Format Specifications & Edge Cases in MPCORB.DAT

Property Specification / Behavior in MPCORB.DAT
Column Positions Columns 118–122 (1-indexed), corresponding to 0-based substring slice [117..122] (length 5 characters), preceded by a space at column 117 (after the 9-character Reference field in cols 108–116) and followed by a space at column 123 (before Nopp in cols 124–126).
FORTRAN Format Descriptor I5 (5-character right-justified base-10 integer, e.g., " 27", " 7361", "11432").
Blank Field (" ") Occasionally left blank (5 spaces) for historical or preliminary orbits imported from external sources where the exact observation count was omitted; parsers should handle whitespace-only slices gracefully (e.g., returning int? = null or 0).
5-Digit Field Ceiling (99999) Because I5 allocates 5 ASCII characters, the maximum representable value in standard fixed-width MPCORB.DAT is 99999 (saturated at 99999 if an object exceeds 99,999 optical observations, whereas extended JSON/ADES catalogs store arbitrary 32-bit integers).

4. Worked Examples from MPCORB.DAT

The following two records from MPCORB.DAT (epoch K25BL = 2025 Nov 21.0 TT = JD 2461000.5) illustrate Nobs in columns 118–122 alongside Nopp (columns 124–126) and the Observation Span (columns 128–136):

Example 1: (1) Ceres — High-Count Multi-Century Dataset (11432)

Excerpt of MPCORB.DAT columns 108–202 for 00001: 

MPO925614 11432  98 1801-2025 0.61 M-v 30h JPL 48     1000   (1) Ceres                  20251018
  • Parsed Nobs (Cols 118–122): "11432" → Nobs = 11,432 accepted optical observations.
  • Degrees of Freedom & Density: Provides νdof = 2(11,432) − 6 = 22,858 scalar constraints across Nopp = 98 oppositions (an average of ≈ 116.7 observations per opposition).

Example 2: Single-Opposition Discovery — Low-Count Short Arc ( 27)

Excerpt of MPCORB.DAT columns 108–202 for a newly discovered NEO: 

E2025-R84    27   1    14 days 0.28 M-v 38k MPCLINUX   8403            2025 QA14          20250911
  • Parsed Nobs (Cols 118–122): " 27" → Nobs = 27 accepted optical observations.
  • Degrees of Freedom & Density: Provides νdof = 2(27) − 6 = 48 scalar constraints over a 14-day single-opposition arc (≈ 1.93 observations/day).
