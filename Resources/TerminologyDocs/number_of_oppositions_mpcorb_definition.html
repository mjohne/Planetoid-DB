Number of Oppositions (Nopp, Columns 124–126)

Synodic Apparition Count (FORTRAN I3), IAU Numbering Criteria & Span Format Discriminator in MPCORB.DAT

1. Formal Definition

In the Minor Planet Center Orbit Database (MPCORB), the Number of Oppositions (Nopp, officially specified by the IAU Minor Planet Center as the “Number of oppositions” field in columns 124–126, 1-indexed, right-justified integer formatted as FORTRAN I3, with 0-based substring slice [123..126]) counts the number of distinct observational apparitions (synodic visibility windows)—separated by solar conjunctions—during which accepted astrometric observations of the minor planet were recorded and included in the orbit solution:

Nopp = int(Record[123 : 126]) ∈ ℕ1,    Psyn = 1|1 / P⊕ − 1 / Past| (Eq. 1)

where:

  • Synodic Apparition Meaning: Although the astronomical term opposition strictly denotes the geometric configuration where a superior body's geocentric ecliptic longitude differs from the Sun's by Δλ = λast − λ⊙ = 180°, in MPC nomenclature Nopp denotes the number of distinct observational apparitions (observing seasons bounded by solar-elongation conjunction gaps). For inferior/Atira objects (a < 1 au) or high-eccentricity NEAs observed near elongation maxima rather than 180° opposition, each separated apparition increments Nopp by 1.
  • Synodic Period (Psyn): For an exterior minor planet with sidereal orbital period Past = a3/2 yr (a > 1 au), successive solar oppositions recur at the synodic interval Psyn = (1 − a−3/2)−1 yr (e.g., Psyn ≈ 1.28 yr for a main-belt asteroid at a = 2.7 au, and Psyn ≈ 1.004 yr for a distant Kuiper-belt object at a = 42 au).
  • Format Switch Controller for Columns 128–136: Within MPCORB.DAT, Nopp directly governs the encoding of the immediately following Observation Span field (columns 128–136): Nopp = 1 selects the integer day format (NNNNN days), whereas Nopp ≥ 2 selects the calendar year-range format (YYYY-YYYY).

2. Astrodynamical Significance & Catalog Governance

  • Why Nopp Is Orthogonal to Nobs: A single-opposition asteroid observed intensively during a close Earth flyby may accumulate Nobs = 500 observations over 20 days (Nopp = 1), yet its semi-major axis a and orbital period P remain substantially less constrained than an object with only Nobs = 24 observations distributed across Nopp = 4 distinct oppositions. Sampling multiple orbital phases around the heliocentric ellipse decorrelates eccentricity e, argument of perihelion ω, and mean motion n.
  • Permanent IAU Numbering Thresholds: Under the rules of the IAU Working Group for Small Bodies Nomenclature (WGSBN) and Minor Planet Center:
    • Main-Belt & Distant Minor Planets: Typically require Nopp ≥ 4 well-observed oppositions (with well-constrained perihelion/aphelion geometry and Condition Code U ≤ 1) before receiving a permanent sequential catalog number (columns 1–7 in MPCORB.DAT).
    • Near-Earth Asteroids (NEAs): May be numbered once observed across Nopp ≥ 2 oppositions (or during a single apparition if high-precision planetary radar astrometry eliminates orbital covariance).
  • Structural Coupling with Columns 128–136: Programmatic parsers of MPCORB.DAT use Nopp (columns 124–126) as the primary discriminator for interpreting columns 128–136:
    Nopp = 1 ⇒ Span = I5(Ndays) days,    Nopp ≥ 2 ⇒ Span = YYYY1-YYYY2 (Eq. 2)

3. Fixed-Width Format Specifications & Edge Cases in MPCORB.DAT

Property Specification / Behavior in MPCORB.DAT
Column Positions Columns 124–126 (1-indexed), corresponding to 0-based substring slice [123..126] (length 3 characters), preceded by a space at column 123 (after Nobs in cols 118–122) and followed by a space at column 127 (before Observation Span in cols 128–136).
FORTRAN Format Descriptor I3 (3-character right-justified base-10 integer, e.g., " 1", " 14", " 98", up to "999").
Minimum Value (Nopp = 1) Every cataloged orbit in MPCORB.DAT has at least Nopp = 1 (formatted as " 1"), even if all observations occurred on a single UTC night ( 0 days).
Maximum Historical Values The earliest discovered minor planets (e.g., (1) Ceres, (2) Pallas, (4) Vesta, (433) Eros) have Nopp ≈ 95–125 (well within the I3 ceiling of 999, which will not saturate until the 29th century).

4. Worked Examples from MPCORB.DAT

The following two records from MPCORB.DAT (epoch K25BL = 2025 Nov 21.0 TT = JD 2461000.5) illustrate Nopp in columns 124–126 and its direct control over columns 128–136:

Example 1: (1) Ceres — 98 Observed Oppositions Since 1801 ( 98)

Excerpt of MPCORB.DAT columns 108–202 for 00001: 

MPO925614 11432  98 1801-2025 0.61 M-v 30h JPL 48     1000   (1) Ceres                  20251018
  • Parsed Nopp (Cols 124–126): " 98" → Nopp = 98 oppositions.
  • Synodic Consistency Check: For Ceres (a = 2.766 au, Past = 4.60 yr), the synodic opposition period is Psyn = (1 − 2.766−1.5)−1 = 1.278 yr. Over the 224-year interval 1801-2025, 224 / 1.278 ≈ 175 geometric oppositions occurred, of which Nopp = 98 apparitions yielded accepted astrometric observations.

Example 2: (99942) Apophis — 14 Observed Oppositions ( 14)

Excerpt of MPCORB.DAT columns 108–202 for 99942: 

MPO618902  7361  14 2004-2021 0.34 M-v 38k JPL 218    1D02   (99942) Apophis            20210512
  • Parsed Nopp (Cols 124–126): " 14" → Nopp = 14 distinct apparitions between 2004 and 2021 (2004-2021), providing a dense multi-apparition baseline that pins down Apophis's post-2029 Earth-encounter trajectory.
