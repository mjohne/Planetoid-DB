Alternate Readable Designation

Name-First Reordering ("Name (N)"), Unparenthesized JPL Form & Principal Provisional Discovery Aliases

1. Formal Definition

In the Minor Planet Center Orbit Database (MPCORB) ecosystem and extended minor-planet catalog schemas, the Alternate Readable Designation (Desigalt) is a derived, canonicalized, or cross-referenced human-readable identifier constructed alongside the primary Readable Designation (columns 167–194, FORTRAN A28) to resolve structural ambiguities in name-based search, alphabetical sorting, and multi-designation linkage:

Desigalt(r) = 𝑇alt( Desigread(r), Desigpacked(r) ) (Eq. 1)

where:

  • Architectural Distinction (Derived/Normalized View vs. Raw Column 167–194): In the strict 202-column IAU MPCORB.DAT flat file, there is only a single 28-character Readable Designation field (columns 167–194, e.g., (1) Ceres or 2025 QA14). In astronomical catalogs, MPC web/JSON exports (mpcorb_extended.json), and orbital analysis software, Alternate Readable Designation refers to the complementary human-readable representation that exposes the other side of an object's nomenclature identity:
    1. Name-First / Unparenthesized Normalization (for Numbered Minor Planets): Stripping or reordering the parenthesized permanent number (N) from (N) Name so the object is indexed directly by its IAU proper name or unadorned number—i.e., "Ceres (1)" or "Ceres" / "1 Ceres" instead of "(1) Ceres"—enabling direct alphabetical lookup by proper name rather than numeric prefix.
    2. Principal / Secondary Provisional Discovery Designations (for Numbered & Multi-Designation Objects): Providing the historical provisional discovery designation(s) under which a numbered asteroid was originally discovered and observed prior to numbering—for example, 1930 SH for (1176) Lucidor, 2004 MN4 for (99942) Apophis, 2003 UB313 for (136199) Eris, or A801 AA for (1) Ceres.
  • Role in Catalog Cross-Identification: Because minor planets frequently accumulate multiple provisional designations across independent survey detections before orbit linkage (e.g., (99942) Apophis = 2004 MN4), retaining an Alternate Readable Designation column alongside the primary Readable Designation ensures zero-ambiguity resolution whether a user or external ephemeris service (JPL Horizons, MPC Explorer, Lowell Astorb, SkyBot) queries by permanent number, IAU proper name, or historical provisional code.

3. Fixed-Width File Context & Software Engineering Semantics

  • Not a Separate Byte Slice in MPCORB.DAT: In the classic 202-character MPCORB.DAT format, only a single 28-character designation field exists at columns 167–194 (0-based [166..194]). Therefore, Alternate Readable Designation is either:
    • Synthesized deterministically at parse time from columns 167–194 (transforming "(N) Name" ↔ "Name (N)" or "N Name"), or
    • Populated from the MPC's extended JSON distribution (mpcorb_extended.json), which separates Number, Name, Principal_desig, and Other_desigs into distinct attributes.
  • Why Both Primary and Alternate Columns Coexist in Virtual Tables: Sorting a table of 1.5 million minor planets by raw Readable Designation ("(1) Ceres", "(2) Pallas", …, "(99942) Apophis") orders rows numerically because every numbered entry starts with '('. Providing an Alternate Readable Designation column formatted as "Name (N)" (e.g., "Ceres (1)", "Pallas (2)") allows users to sort and incrementally search by proper name (e.g., typing "Vesta" jumps straight to "Vesta (4)") without losing the permanent catalog number.

4. Worked Examples from MPCORB.DAT

The following two records from MPCORB.DAT (epoch K25BL = 2025 Nov 21.0 TT = JD 2461000.5) illustrate how Readable Designation (columns 167–194) maps to its Alternate Readable Designation forms:

Example 1: (1) Ceres — Numbered & Named Minor Planet

Excerpt of MPCORB.DAT columns 151–202 for 00001: 

JPL 48     1000   (1) Ceres                  20251018
  • Primary Readable Designation (Cols 167–194): "(1) Ceres"
  • Alternate Readable Designations:
    • Name-First Lexicographic Form: "Ceres (1)"
    • JPL / Unparenthesized Form: "1 Ceres"
    • Historical Discovery Provisional Alias: "A801 AA" (discovered 1801 Jan 1 by G. Piazzi)

Example 2: 2025 QA14 — Unnumbered Provisional Minor Planet

Excerpt of MPCORB.DAT columns 151–202 for K25Q14A: 

MPCLINUX   8403            2025 QA14          20250911
  • Primary Readable Designation (Cols 167–194): "2025 QA14"
  • Alternate Readable Designations:
    • Name-First Form: "2025 QA14" (invariant, as no permanent number or proper name is assigned yet)
    • Compact / Packed-Equivalent Form: "2025QA14" (packed key in cols 1–7: "K25Q14A")
