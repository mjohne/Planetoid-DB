Apsidal Distance Difference (Δraps = Q − q = 2ae) in the Context of the MPCORB Database

Scientific Definition, Radial Excursion Amplitude of Bound Orbits, and Computation from MPCORB

1. Fundamental Astrodynamical & Geometric Definition

Definition & Nomenclature: In two-body Keplerian celestial mechanics, the apsidal distance difference (also termed total radial excursion, apsidal span, or inter-apsidal radial amplitude, denoted Δraps or Q − q) is the difference between the maximum heliocentric distance at aphelion (Q = a(1 + e)) and the minimum heliocentric distance at perihelion (q = a(1 − e)) on a bound elliptic orbit (0 ≤ e < 1), expressed in Astronomical Units (AU) or kilometers (km).

Subtracting the perihelion radius from the aphelion radius yields an exact linear relation in both semi-major axis a and orbital eccentricity e:

Δraps ≡ Q − q = a(1 + e) − a(1 − e) = 2ae = 2c

where c = ae = √(a2 − b2) is the linear eccentricity (distance from the center of the ellipse to either focus). Thus, the apsidal distance difference equals exactly twice the focal offset (2c), or twice the radial oscillation amplitude Ar = ae about the mean orbital distance a = ½(Q + q).

Geometric & Kinematic Properties

  • Vanishing in the Circular Limit: For a circular orbit (e = 0), Δraps = 0 AU; for any non-circular bound ellipse (0 < e < 1), 0 < Δraps < 2a.
  • Inverse Reconstruction of a and e: Together with the major axis 2a = Q + q, Δraps recovers orbital eccentricity directly as e = (Q − q) / (Q + q) = Δraps / (2a).
  • Work-Energy & Potential Variation: Across one half-period from aphelion to perihelion, the heliocentric gravitational potential energy drops by ΔU = −μ⊙(1/q − 1/Q) = −μ⊙ Δraps / (qQ) = −2μ⊙e / [a(1 − e2)], converting directly into kinetic energy gain ½(vq2 − vQ2).
  • Non-Existence for Unbound Orbits (e ≥ 1): Because parabolic (e = 1) and hyperbolic (e > 1) trajectories have no finite aphelion (Q → ∞), Δraps is strictly defined only for bound elliptic orbits (0 ≤ e < 1).

3. Specific Status and Derivation in the MPCORB Catalog

  • Implicit Derived Quantity (Not Tabulated): MPCORB.DAT does not store Δraps, q, or Q. It is computed directly as 2ae from eccentricity e (columns 71–79, F9.7) and semi-major axis a (columns 93–103, F11.7, AU).
  • Numerical Superiority of 2.0 * a * e over Q - q: Computing Δraps = 2ae directly avoids catastrophic floating-point cancellation when e ≪ 1 (near-circular orbits where Q ≈ q) and executes in two multiplications without forming Q or q first.
  • Planetary-Zone Crossing Indicator: Large Δraps identifies bodies that traverse multiple planetary regimes (e.g., centaurs, active asteroids, and Jupiter-family comets whose orbits span from the inner terrestrial region out to Jupiter or beyond).
