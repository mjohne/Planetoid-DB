Coarse Indicator of Perturbers (Columns 143–145)

Planetary Perturbation Span Mnemonic (FORTRAN A3: "M-v") in MPCORB.DAT

1. Formal Definition

In the Minor Planet Center Orbit Database (MPCORB), the Coarse Indicator of Perturbers (officially specified by the IAU Minor Planet Center as the “Coarse indicator of perturbers” field in columns 143–145, 1-indexed, 3-character left-justified ASCII string formatted as FORTRAN A3, with 0-based substring slice [142..145]) is a compact mnemonic code denoting the planetary and massive minor-planet N-body gravitational force model utilized during the numerical integration and differential least-squares orbit determination:

Perturberscoarse = Record[142 : 145] ∈ {"M-v", "M-h", " ", …} (Eq. 1)

where:

  • Physical Meaning in the Equations of Motion: In high-precision solar-system orbit determination, the heliocentric acceleration of a minor planet 𝑟̈ is integrated under the Newtonian N-body and post-Newtonian relativistic equations of motion:
    𝑟̈ = −μ⊙ 𝑟 / r3 + ∑j ∈ 𝒫 μj ( (𝑟j − 𝑟) / |𝑟j − 𝑟|3 − 𝑟j / rj3 ) + 𝑎rel + 𝑎non-grav (Eq. 2)
    Here, 𝒫 is the set of active perturbing bodies (planetary ephemeris + massive asteroids). The 3-character coarse indicator (cols 143–145) summarizes the planetary span of 𝒫, while the immediately following 3-character Precise indicator of perturbers (cols 147–149, e.g., 30h or 38k) encodes the exact planetary ephemeris and massive-perturber configuration in hexadecimal/base-36 notation.
  • Field Delimitation: Columns 143–145 are preceded by a space at column 142 (after the RMS residual in columns 138–141) and followed by a space at column 146 (before the Precise indicator of perturbers in columns 147–149).

2. Planetary Mnemonic & Coupling with Columns 147–149

Code (Cols 143–145) Mnemonic Expansion Dynamical Force-Model Interpretation
M-v Mercury through Neverrier / Neptune Full planetary N-body integration spanning all eight major planets from Mercury (M) to Neptune (historically denoted by lowercase v for Le Verrier, the co-discoverer of Neptune, alongside Pluto and the major perturbing main-belt asteroids such as Ceres, Pallas, and Vesta as specified in columns 147–149).
M-h Mercury through herschel (Uranus) Legacy planetary integration spanning Mercury through Uranus (h = Herschel), omitting Neptune and trans-Neptunian perturbers.
V-n / E-n Venus or Earth through neptune Historical approximation used in early mainframe integrations of outer main-belt asteroids where Mercury's mass was folded into the solar gravitational parameter μ⊙.
    (3 spaces) Unperturbed / Two-Body Pure Keplerian two-body orbit (𝒫 = ∅) without planetary perturbations—characteristic of preliminary single-opposition discovery tracklets (Nobs = 2–3) or circular/Väisälä initial orbits.

Coupling with the Precise Indicator of Perturbers (Columns 147–149)

Whereas columns 143–145 give a human-readable 3-character summary (M-v), columns 147–149 (0-based [146..149]) encode the exact bitmask and ephemeris version:

  • M-v 30h: Full planetary perturbations from JPL DE430/DE431 plus the 16/24 massive minor-planet perturbers (standard for recent JPL Horizons / MPC updates).
  • M-v 38k: Full planetary perturbations plus the 300+ asteroid perturber model used by the MPC's MPCLINUX / ORBFIT production pipeline.
  • M-v 00h: Full planetary perturbations (Mercury–Neptune + Pluto) with zero massive minor-planet perturbers included.

3. Fixed-Width Format Specifications & Parsing Rules

  • Exact Column Slice: Columns 143–145 (1-indexed), corresponding to 0-based substring line[142..145] (length 3 characters).
  • FORTRAN Format Descriptor: A3 (3-character ASCII string), bounded by ASCII spaces at column 142 and column 146.
  • Case Sensitivity: Case-sensitive! The first character is uppercase (M = Mercury) while the terminal planet character is lowercase (v = Neptune/Le Verrier, h = Uranus/Herschel). Parsers must preserve exact case when comparing against "M-v".

Example 1: (1) Ceres — Full Planetary + Massive Asteroid Perturbers (M-v + 30h)

Excerpt of MPCORB.DAT columns 108–202 for 00001: 

MPO925614 11432  98 1801-2025 0.61 M-v 30h JPL 48     1000   (1) Ceres                  20251018
  • Parsed Coarse Indicator (Cols 143–145): "M-v" (Mercury through Neptune/Pluto).
  • Force-Model Context: Combined with "30h" (cols 147–149) and "JPL 48" (cols 151–160), indicating a full solar-system N-body integration including mutual perturbations among the massive main-belt asteroids.

Example 2: 2025 QA14 — MPC Production Pipeline Integration (M-v + 38k)

Excerpt of MPCORB.DAT columns 108–202 for 2025 QA14: 

E2025-R84    27   1    14 days 0.28 M-v 38k MPCLINUX   8403            2025 QA14          20250911
  • Parsed Coarse Indicator (Cols 143–145): "M-v" (Mercury through Neptune/Pluto).
  • Force-Model Context: Paired with "38k" (cols 147–149) and "MPCLINUX" (cols 151–160), confirming that even this 14-day discovery arc was integrated using full planetary plus asteroid-belt N-body perturbations rather than a two-body approximation.
